# 01 Understand — SKF humanoid training

SKF workshop engineers struggle to deploy humanoids for shop-floor work because training locomotion that survives stairs, obstacles and slippery floors is slow, brittle and poorly documented, resulting in pilots that never reach real production.

No technology named above, per checkpoint. What blocks them is not a missing
algorithm: it is the lack of a documented, repeatable pipeline that takes a
locomotion skill from idea to a simulation test anyone can re-run and reason
about. That missing pipeline is exactly what the providers asked for.
